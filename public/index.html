DNS Record Converter

Convert hosts records and BIND zones locally in your browser.

Example: safesploit.com or example.com
Supports octet prefixes and aligned /8, /16 or /24 CIDR. Example: 172.16. or 192.0.2.0/24.
Short label or absolute name. Full zones need an A record for an in-zone nameserver.
DNS mailbox notation, such as admin or hostmaster.example.com., not an email address.
Incremented after each successful generation. Supply the installed serial before applying a change.
Include output has absolute names and per-record TTLs. Use it inside a zone with $INCLUDE.
Format: IP primary-host alias1 alias2. Primary hostname becomes an A record. Extra hostnames become CNAMEs by default.
